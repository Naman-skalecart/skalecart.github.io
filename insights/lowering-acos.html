Why Lowering ACOS Isn't Always A Win

6 min read·Advertising

ACOS is one of the most watched metrics in Amazon advertising—and one of the most misunderstood. A lower ACOS feels like a win. Often it is. But not always.

The Problem With Optimizing For ACOS Alone

When teams chase a lower ACOS in isolation, they often cut spend on campaigns that were actually profitable at scale, just because the percentage looked worse. The result: lower ACOS, but also lower total profit.

What Matters More

Total profitable revenue matters more than the ratio. A campaign running at a 25% ACOS that drives significant profitable volume can be far more valuable than one at 10% ACOS generating a fraction of the sales.

How We Think About It

We look at ACOS alongside contribution margin, incremental revenue and long-term customer value—not as a standalone target. The right ACOS depends entirely on your margins and growth stage.
