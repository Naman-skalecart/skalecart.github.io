What We Look For In The First 30 Minutes Of An Audit

5 min read·Marketplace Strategy

Experienced consultants can spot the biggest issues fast. It's not magic—it's pattern recognition from reviewing hundreds of accounts. Here's exactly what we check first, and why.

1. Pricing And Margin Structure

Before anything else, we check whether pricing actually supports profitable operations at current ad spend and fulfillment costs. This single number reframes everything else we look at.

2. Advertising Efficiency vs Organic Mix

We look at how much of total sales are ad-driven versus organic. A business overly dependent on ads has a very different risk profile than one with strong organic demand.

3. Inventory Health

Stockout history and aging inventory tell us a lot about how well-forecasted and disciplined the operation is—often more than the founder realizes.

4. Review Trends

Rating trajectory and recent review sentiment usually reveal product or fulfillment issues before they show up clearly in the sales data.

These four checks alone usually surface 70-80% of what ends up in the final roadmap. Everything after that is about depth and prioritization.
